<h2 align="center">About</h2>

I'm a full-stack developer from Tajikistan. I build complete products — React interfaces, NestJS APIs, PostgreSQL data models and Android apps — and take them from the first idea to production.

My recent work is business software: a CRM for markets and shops, a platform for educational centers, and a CRM for schools and courses. I care about maintainable code, thoughtful user experience and solving concrete problems with software.
